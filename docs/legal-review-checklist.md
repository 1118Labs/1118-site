# Internal legal-review checklist

This checklist is internal launch evidence. The public policy pages are not represented as attorney-approved.

## Required before Production

- Review the Privacy page against the final hosting, analytics, cookie, and email configuration. The website has no contact form or active contact backend.
- Review the Terms language on ownership, permission, governing law, warranties, and limitation of liability.
- Confirm permission to publish the four exact SkyPups photographs and demonstration testimonials used by the Reviews Engine carousel on the 1118 company site.
- Confirm the Etchr spokesmodel release and publication scope.
- Confirm Signal transaction-publicity, archival screenshot rights, and publishability of the visible structure and metric identifiers. The protected Preview follows Steve’s supplied wording; Production remains gated.
- Keep the acquirer, transaction terms, client data, signatures, exact trading configurations, and non-public documents off the website.
- Keep Playbook off the public site until media and third-party rights are cleared.

## Product-language boundaries

- Etchr: live and available on the App Store.
- Reviews Engine: live; the carousel is explicitly labeled as demonstration reviews.
- Property Insights: in development; the authentic product interface is populated with an explicitly local simulation fixture and publicly labeled as demonstration data.
- Signal: historical, built, licensed, and acquired; no performance claims.
- Playbook: founder-created product concept developed toward launch; never describe it as launched, acquired, or commercially operating.
