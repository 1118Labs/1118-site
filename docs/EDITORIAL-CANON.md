5. PORTRAIT CASE STUDY
==================================================

Preserve the strong source-derived narrative structure but update it for the current product.

Use these sections:

THE GAP

Most AI-generated portraits still look unmistakably generated—too synthetic, too stylized, or too far from the person in the original photograph.

THE IDEA

Create an editorial portrait from one real photograph while preserving the person, expression, and character that made the photograph theirs.

WHAT WE BUILT

Portrait takes users from source photograph to finished editorial portrait, then prepares that portrait for the places people actually use it—profiles, websites, social media, and print.

PRODUCT OUTCOME

A finished portrait that feels composed enough to publish and familiar enough to still feel like you.

12. HOW WE BUILD — REPLACE CURRENT OPERATING MODEL
==================================================

The Shipyard concept is directionally correct, but the language needs refinement for 1118.

Headline:

FROM IDEA
TO WORKING PRODUCT.

Intro:

We move from an idea worth pursuing to something people can actually use.

Then four steps:

01

FIND THE OPPORTUNITY

See what is missing, broken, or harder than it should be.

02

DEFINE THE PRODUCT

Turn the idea into a clear proposition, experience, and operating model.

03

BUILD IT

Bring design, software, AI, and infrastructure together into a working product.

04

LAUNCH AND LEARN

Put it into the world, see what happens, and keep making it better.

Do not use:

Spot what is missing.

Shape the product.

From product gap to launched system.

Platform in place of product.

“Platform” is too narrow for the company-level operating model.
